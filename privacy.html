This policy explains exactly what GGS Studio collects when you use this website, why we hold it, who else can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We only collect what a booking actually needs. We do not sell your information, we do not run advertising trackers, and we do not profile you.

1. Who we are

GGS Studio is a recording studio at Manson Trading, Looc, Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu, Philippines. For anything covered by this policy we are the personal information controller under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Reach us at ggs.studio2026@gmail.com or +63 976 350 6301.

2. What we collect, and why

Everything below is collected because a specific part of booking or paying for a session needs it. Nothing here is optional-but-collected-anyway.

WhatWhy we need it
Account details — your name, email address, and password. A phone number if you choose to add one. An account is optional: you can book without one. To create your account, sign you in, attach bookings to you, and contact you about a session. Passwords are stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider — we never see or store the password itself.
Booking without an account — the name and email you type on the booking form, plus a random identifier and a random key generated by your browser and kept in its local storage. We store the identifier and only a one-way hash of the key. To hold the booking in your name, and to let that browser prove a booking is yours so you can view, reschedule, or cancel it without signing in. The identifier is not linked to advertising and is never shared. If you later sign in or register with the same email address, those bookings move into your account. Clearing your browser's site data erases both values, and with them that browser's access to those bookings.
Booking details — the date, start and end time, the room, the add-on services you picked and how many units of each, the calculated price, the booking status, and any note you or our staff add. To hold the slot, price the session correctly, and manage changes, cancellations, and the studio calendar.
Photo of a valid ID — only when you choose to pay cash at the studio. To hold a slot that has not been paid for yet. It is stored in a private, access-controlled bucket that only you and studio staff can open.
Payment records — the amount, the payment option (cash, downpayment, or full), the channel used (GCash, GoTyme, or BPI), the reference number you enter, your uploaded receipt image, and whether staff verified or rejected it (with the reason). To confirm your booking, keep an accurate record of what was paid, handle refunds, and meet our bookkeeping and tax obligations. We never see or store your card number, bank login, or e-wallet password.
Chat assistant messages — what you type into the help chat at the bottom-right of the site. To answer your question. See section 4 for exactly what leaves our systems when a question is complex enough to need our AI provider.
Technical records — your sign-in session token (kept in your own browser's local storage), plus standard server logs such as IP address, timestamps, and error traces held by our hosting provider. To keep you signed in, to keep the service secure, and to diagnose faults and abuse. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics pixels, or cross-site trackers.

Our lawful basis

  • Performing our contract with you — account, booking, and payment data. Without it we cannot provide the session you asked for.
  • Legal obligation — keeping payment and receipt records for tax and accounting.
  • Legitimate interests — securing the site, preventing fraudulent or abusive bookings, and fixing faults.
  • Your consent — the optional phone number, and anything you volunteer in a booking note or a chat message. You can withdraw it by removing the information or contacting us.

3. Who can see your information

Inside the studio, only GGS Studio staff and administrators can see customer records, and only through the staff dashboard. Access to the database is restricted per-account by row-level security, so a signed-in customer can only ever read their own bookings, payments, ID photo, and receipts. Uploaded ID photos and receipts sit in private storage and are opened by staff through short-lived links, not permanent public URLs.

Outside the studio, we share only what a service needs to do its job:

ProviderWhat they process
Supabase — database, sign-in, file storage, and server functions. All of the data in section 2. This is where your information actually lives.
Google (Google AI Studio / Gemini) — the chat assistant. Only your chat messages, your first name, and a summary of your own bookings. Your email address, phone number, password, ID photo, and receipt images are never sent.
Our email provider — booking and account emails. Your email address and the contents of the message being sent to you.
GitHub Pages — hosting for this website. Standard web request logs. No booking or account data is stored there.
PayMongo — card and e-wallet checkout, currently switched off. Nothing at present. If online card/e-wallet checkout is re-enabled, the amount and a booking reference would be sent to them to process the payment. Any card details would be entered on their page, never ours.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it — for example a lawful order from a court or government agency. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

4. The chat assistant

The help chat at the bottom-right of the site answers most questions on its own, using information the page already has — our published rates, opening hours, our booking and payment policy, and, if you are signed in, your own bookings. Those answers never leave your browser and are not sent anywhere.

When a question is too open-ended for a prepared answer, we forward it to Google's Gemini model to write a reply. In that case we send: the recent messages in that conversation, your first name, and a short summary of your own bookings (room, date and time, status, total, add-ons, and payment state). We do not send your email address, phone number, ID photo, or receipt images. Our API key stays on our server and is never exposed to your browser.

The assistant is limited to GGS Studio topics and cannot make, change, cancel, or pay for a booking — only you can, through the booking form and the My Bookings page. Its answers are a convenience and are not a binding quotation; the price shown on the booking form, and the terms you accept when you book, are what govern your session. Please do not type sensitive personal details, passwords, or card numbers into the chat.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account details — for as long as your account is open.
  • Bookings and payment records — for as long as needed to run the studio and to satisfy tax and accounting requirements, even after a session has passed.
  • ID photos and receipt images — kept only while they are needed to hold or verify the booking they belong to, and erased when you delete your account.
  • Chat messages — kept only in your browser for the length of that conversation. We do not store chat transcripts on our servers.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself from your Profile page, or ask our staff to do it. Deleting an account permanently erases your login, your personal details, and any ID photos and receipts you uploaded.

Past sessions themselves are kept for the studio's books, but are anonymised — they stay on record as walk-in bookings with no link back to you. This is deliberate: we cannot delete a financial record we are required to keep, so we strip the personal information out of it instead. Deletion is refused only while you still have an upcoming session on the calendar or money that has not been refunded; resolve those first, or ask us and we will sort it out with you.

7. Your rights under the Data Privacy Act

As a data subject under RA 10173 you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how your information is collected and used — that is what this policy is for.
  • Access a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Correct anything inaccurate or out of date. Your name and phone number are editable from your Profile page at any time.
  • Object to processing, and withdraw any consent you have given.
  • Erasure or blocking of your information, subject to section 6.
  • Data portability — receive your information in a usable electronic format.
  • Damages for a violation of your rights, and to lodge a complaint.

To exercise any of these, email ggs.studio2026@gmail.com. We will respond promptly and, where a request needs more work, within the periods set by law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic to this site and to our database is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Row-level security is enforced in the database, so one customer's records are unreachable from another customer's account.
  • ID photos and payment receipts live in private storage buckets, not public URLs, and staff open them through short-lived signed links.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed by our authentication provider; nobody at GGS Studio can read them.
  • Secret keys for third-party services are held server-side only and are never sent to your browser.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as the law requires.

9. Children

This site is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. Minors should book through a parent or guardian, who is responsible for the booking and for everyone they bring to the session. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.
